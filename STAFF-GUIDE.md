# D'Lish Staff Guide

Welcome to the D'Lish website dashboard! This guide explains how to update your menu, manage what's trending, and update your shop's settings.

## Accessing the Dashboard
Go to: `www.your-website.com/studio` (Requires login)

## 1. Updating the Menu

### Adding a New Category
1. Go to **Categories**.
2. Click **Create New Category**.
3. Give it a name (e.g., "Samosas & Snacks").
4. Important: Set the **Order Number**. (e.g., 1 for Bubble Tea, 2 for Milkshakes). This dictates the order on the Menu page.

### Adding a New Item (Food/Drink)
1. Go to **Products**.
2. Click **Create New Product**.
3. Fill in the **Name**, **Description**, and **Price** (Format: 500 = £5.00).
4. Upload a gorgeous **Photo** (square recommended).
5. Select the **Category** it belongs to.
6. **Allergens & Dietary**: Check any allergens present and toggle tags like "Vegetarian" or "Vegan".
7. Click **Publish**. The website updates instantly.

## 2. Managing "Trending" & "Popular"

Your website is smart! You don't need to manually build the "Popular" or "Trending" sections on the homepage and menu.
- **To make an item appear in "Popular"**: Simply open the product and toggle the `Popular` switch to ON.
- **To make an item appear in "Trending at D'Lish"**: Open the product and add the `TRENDING`, `NEW`, or `BESTSELLER` tag in the "Badges" field. It will automatically be promoted to the homepage carousel.

## 3. Selling Out / Turning Off Items
Ran out of Tapioca Pearls or Samosas?
1. Open the Product.
2. Toggle the `Available` switch to **OFF**.
3. Click Publish.
4. The item will now appear greyed out with a "SOLD OUT" badge on the live website. You don't have to delete it!

## 4. Modifying Bubble Tea Options
Want to change the price of popping boba or add a new flavour?
1. Go to **Bubble Tea Options**.
2. Here you can edit the Flavour lists, Sweetness levels, Ice levels, and Toppings.
3. If you increase the price of a topping (e.g., from 50p to 75p), the website's customiser will automatically calculate the new total price instantly for the customer.

## 5. Store Settings (Opening Hours & Ordering)
Need to change your opening hours for a bank holiday or switch the ordering method?
1. Go to **Site Settings**.
2. **Order Link Type**: Choose where the "ORDER NOW" buttons send the customer. You can pick `WhatsApp`, `Phone`, or `URL` (e.g., Deliveroo).
3. Update your **Opening Hours** or **Phone Number** here. It will automatically update the "Find Us" page and the footer across the entire site.

---
*Tip: Changes usually appear on the website within a few seconds after hitting "Publish". If they don't, try refreshing the website page.*
